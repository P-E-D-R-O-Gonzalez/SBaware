SB Aware is built by [Pedro Gonzalez](https://entregonzalez.com/) who works at the intersection of AI, cybersecurity, computer science, and social impact. Driven by purpose and resilience, Pedro is committed to empowering underrepresented students in STEM and underserved communities. He prioritizes students and real impact.

I built SB Aware to make local information and civic participation easier to access. The platform is designed to be replicated by other cities, with low launch and maintenance costs and a no-code CMS that anyone can manage.

Our content draws from a mix of sources, including the City of San Bernardino, local community organizations, the Inland Empire, and surrounding regions.

SB Aware is an independent project and is not operated by or affiliated with the City of San Bernardino.

To submit a correction or request, please use our [digital letterbox](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdtslIAUz7MsyI_mm3v1BRztajx2PhErNVToVO2BsMLprInMA/viewform?usp=header).
